# Privacy Notice for QuellPass

Effective: October 1, 2026

[Deutsche Fassung](PRIVACY.md)

QuellPass by Olaf Wulf contains instructions for research and source verification and an optional local tool for ranking text sections already retrieved. QuellPass does not operate its own server, database, analytics, or tracking services.

Using QuellPass does not give Olaf Wulf access to your questions, texts, search results, evidence records, or personal data. The package does not independently transmit these contents to the publisher. The publisher receives and retains no usage data from operation of the plugin.

The AI application processes input and output under its provider's terms and privacy policies. QuellPass does not change that processing or the retention periods applicable there.

For research, the AI application's search tool sends search terms to the search engines or search services used. Opening original sources involves requests to the relevant websites. The services used and their processing and retention practices depend on the application and its configuration. Personal information and confidential text passages should not be included in external search requests.

Evidence records and research reports may be retained in the conversation or saved as files in the user's environment on request. These contents are not sent to Olaf Wulf. Their location, access, and retention depend on the application and the user's choices.

The public version includes neither an embedding model nor a local vector-ranking tool. The host AI compares retrieved text passages by meaning; these assessments are model judgments, not vector measurements.

QuellPass is also intended to help young people research and verify sources. The age requirements of the AI application and search services used apply.

When contacting the publisher voluntarily or making public GitHub posts, the publisher receives the information provided there. Please do not publish confidential texts or personal data of third parties.

Contact: olafwulf@msn.com
